About Us

RED ARENA PVP is a competitive FiveM arena server built for pure skill, clean fights, and optimized performance. Our goal is simple — create a fair and intense PvP environment where every shot counts. With a dedicated management team, advanced anti-cheat systems, and continuous updates, we focus on delivering a smooth and balanced gameplay experience for every player. Whether you're here to dominate the leaderboard or improve your skills, RED ARENA is where real fighters play.

Server Rules

Proper Reporting

Proof/clip (video or screenshots) is required for every report. No action is taken without evidence.

Respect Staff Decisions

Follow staff instructions. You can appeal a decision, but disrespect or harassment is not allowed.

No Exploits / Bug Abuse

Do not use glitches, map bugs, invisible spots, hitbox abuse, or animation abuse to gain an advantage.

No Cheats / Third‑Party Tools

Aimbot, ESP, mod menus, macros, injected DLLs, and external crosshair tools are strictly banned.
